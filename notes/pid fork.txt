pid = fork();
```

**After fork(), TWO processes exist:**

### Child Process:
- **Real PID**: 12346 (the actual process ID in the system)
- **Variable `pid`**: 0 (what fork returned to the child)

### Parent Process:
- **Real PID**: 12345 (unchanged)
- **Variable `pid`**: 12346 (the child's real PID)
```
